# Corporate Travel Management (CTM)

> Brisbane-founded, ASX-listed (CTD) travel management company established in 1994. CTM procures,
> books and services corporate, government, meetings and events, resources, sport and leisure travel
> across Australia and New Zealand, North America, Europe and Asia. It sits on the buy side of the
> travel distribution chain: an IATA-accredited agency that aggregates supplier content from GDS,
> direct airline APIs and airline NDC connections and resells it to corporate clients through its
> own proprietary technology stack.

**API posture: closed.** CTM publishes no developer portal, no API documentation and no
machine-readable API contract. There is no OpenAPI, Swagger, GraphQL, AsyncAPI or MCP surface on any
CTM host, no developer sign-up, no sandbox and no partner tier. Access to CTM systems follows from
being a contracted CTM client. This file was generated by API Evangelist from the public record —
CTM does not publish an llms.txt (every /llms.txt probe returns 404).

## Products

- [Lightning](https://au.travelctm.com/technology/lightning/): CTM's proprietary online booking tool, aggregating GDS content, direct low-cost-carrier API connections and live airline NDC offers into one policy-controlled corporate booking flow. Production host: https://booking.lightningbook.com.au/ (customer-gated).
- [CTM Portal](https://au.travelctm.com/technology/travel-portal/): single sign-on customer portal for pre-trip planning, approvals, in-trip tracking, risk alerts and reporting. Live at https://portal.travelctm.com/ and https://www.ctmsmart.com.au/ for existing customers only.
- [CTM Approve](https://au.travelctm.com/technology/pre-trip-approval/): pre-trip approval workflow enforcing corporate travel policy before ticketing.
- [CTM Data Hub](https://au.travelctm.com/technology/travel-reporting/): travel reporting and analytics across air, hotel, car, rail, traveller tracking, carbon and wellbeing. No export, feed or connector is documented.
- [CTM Fare Forecaster](https://au.travelctm.com/technology/travel-forecasting/): predictive fare forecasting.
- [CTM Mobile App](https://au.travelctm.com/technology/ctm-mobile-app/): traveller booking and itinerary app.
- [CTM Scout](https://au.travelctm.com/technology/ctm-scout/): chat-based booking and servicing channel, shipped as an embeddable widget.
- [Travel Risk Management](https://au.travelctm.com/technology/risk-management/): traveller risk alerts and a destination requirements hub.
- [NDC](https://au.travelctm.com/ndc/): CTM consumes live NDC content from Qantas, American Airlines and United Airlines into Lightning. It publishes no NDC endpoint of its own.

## APIs

- CTM Portal host API — https://portal-host.api.ctmsmart.com/api — private, undocumented back end for the CTM Portal, named in the portal's own client bootstrap. Returns "Healthy" on the root; every specification path (/openapi.json, /swagger/v1/swagger.json, /api-docs) returns 404. Bearer-token protected via CTM's Auth0 tenant. No public documentation, no sign-up, no terms permitting third-party use.

## Specs

## Compliance and legal

- [PCI DSS Attestation of Compliance](https://au.travelctm.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Payment-Card-Industry-Data-Security-Standard.pdf): SAQ D – Merchants, PCI DSS v3.2.1, self-assessed April 2021, signed by the Global CIO. Not refreshed to PCI DSS v4.
- [Terms and conditions](https://au.travelctm.com/terms-and-conditions/): self-scoped to "all websites and online travel platforms owned by Corporate Travel Management Limited"; prohibits automated screen capture / screen scraping and prohibits providing information from the site to any third party.
- [Privacy policy](https://au.travelctm.com/privacy/): GDPR/UK/Swiss and California data-portability rights, exercised by email to privacy@travelctm.com or DPO@travelctm.com. This is the only published data-exit route; there is no self-service export.
